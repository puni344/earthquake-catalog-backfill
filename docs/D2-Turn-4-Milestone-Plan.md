For each successful concurrent read record timestamp, publication ID and digest of the rows returned in deterministic order. Compare against the independent expected digest for that ID. Require observed reads before kill, during interruption and after recovery, record failures and recovery time, then query both IDs again and verify their returned rows. Save quake-publish.txt. |
