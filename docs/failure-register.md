The M06 evidence covers the interval after pointer update and before commit. [M02 acquisition faults](evidence/M02-acquire.txt) exercised the acceptance gate: an injected disconnect after 512 real bytes on each bounded attempt retained partial staging files without a completed manifest. Evidence filenames are relative to docs/evidence/. The proposed runner is `python -m earthquake.verify --drill NAME` from the workspace root.
